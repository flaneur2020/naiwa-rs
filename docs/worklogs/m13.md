## Changes

- `attach_prefill` moved from `cpu.rs` to `provider.rs`, its only caller. It is
  not in `load.rs`: `load.rs` selects the provider before the model is loaded,
  while `attach_prefill` needs the per-layer `ExpertLoadContext` and the loaded
  `MoEExperts` that `GpuCacheMoeProvider::build` receives.
- `cpu.rs` and `expert_bytes.rs` merged into `expert.rs`:
  - `CpuExperts`, `FUSED_MAX_TOKENS`
  - `expert_range`, `expert_qtensor_on_device`
- `slot_bytes_from_qtensor` moved to `slot.rs`, next to `Slot`/`Projections`;
  it is the VRAM-per-slot estimate, not byte extraction.
