# M12 — provider-owned staging, Layers beside CachedLayer

Status: **done**.
Parent: [m11.md](m11.md).

## Changes

- Remove staging from `CachedMoe`. `GpuCacheMoeProvider` now owns
  `Arc<StagingRings>` directly; each `CachedLayer` gets an `Arc` clone for its
  DMA and warmup paths.
- Rename `CachedMoe` back to `Layers` and move it into `cached_layer.rs`.
  `cached_moe.rs` is gone. `CachedLayer::cached_moe()` becomes
  `CachedLayer::layers()`; `CachedLayer` also exposes `staging()`.
- Move `fused_decode` out of `cpu.rs` into `stages/cpu_miss.rs`. `cpu.rs` keeps
  `CpuExperts`, `FUSED_MAX_TOKENS` and `attach_prefill`; both `CpuMiss` and
  `CpuExperts::forward` use the stage's fused decode through the parent
  re-export.
